Add a delivery area

Customers who choose delivery pick their area from these buttons (at most 12 active areas, names up to 20 characters) and the fee is added to their total. Other areas lets a customer type an area that isn't listed; the fee then stays to be confirmed and you set it in the Orders tab before confirming the order. Switch Other areas off to deliver only to the listed areas. With no areas switched on, the bot charges its flat ₱50 fee.
